# Up the Divisions - Football Management

A football management game in a single web page. Open `index.html` in any browser,
or on an iPad. There is nothing to install.

Take a part-time club from the Conference, the bottom of a five-division pyramid,
and try to reach the Premier Division. All clubs and players are fictional.

## How it plays

- **Five divisions of 12 clubs.** Each season has 22 league games. The top two
  go up automatically, and 3rd to 6th play off for the last promotion place. The
  bottom three go down.
- **You only know what your staff know.** Every rating is a range, not a number.
  The range narrows the longer a player is at your club, or after you send a scout
  to watch them. The assistant picks the team from the same rough ratings, so it can
  be wrong too.
- **Team selection.** Give each player a role (GK, DF, MF or FW). Your formation
  follows from how many of each you pick. Choose a style: defensive, balanced or
  attacking. Fitness, form and morale all matter.
- **Matchday.** The commentary comes in like an old vidiprinter. Pause at any time,
  or at half-time, to change your style or make up to three substitutions.
- **Transfers.** Scout, bid, loan and sign free agents while the window is open
  (pre-season up to week 12). You have a transfer budget and a wage budget.
  Players at bigger clubs may not want to drop down to your level. Other clubs
  bid for your players too, so you may have to sell your best one.
- **The board.** Each season the board set a target based on how good your squad
  is. Their confidence rises and falls with results. Fall too far and you're sacked.
- **Your career.** After a sacking, other clubs offer you a job, usually a smaller
  one. Do well and your reputation grows, and bigger clubs come looking for you.
- **Seasons roll on.** Players age, improve or decline, and retire. You get two
  youth players every summer.

The game saves as you go, on the device you're playing on.
