# Baskerville Blocks™

Created for Lou Baskerville.

A falling-block puzzle game in a single web page, made for playing on an iPad.
Open `index.html` in any browser. There is nothing to install.

## How to play

Pieces of four squares fall into the well. Move and turn them so they fill rows
from side to side; a full row clears. The game ends when the stack reaches the top.

| Action | iPad | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | ◀ ▶ (hold to slide) | ← → |
| Soft drop (falls faster) | ▼ | ↓ |
| Drop straight down | Drop | Space |
| Turn | ↺ ↻ | Z, X or ↑ |
| Hold a piece for later | Hold | C or Shift |
| Pause | Pause | P or Esc |

## Levels and scoring

- You choose a starting level from 1 to 15. The level goes up by itself as your score
  grows: level 2 at 1,000 points, 3 at 3,000, 4 at 6,000, 5 at 10,000 and so on. The
  pieces fall faster at each level, and the next target is shown under the level.
- Clearing 1, 2, 3 or 4 rows at once scores 100, 300, 500 or 800, times the level.
- Soft drop scores 1 point per row and Drop scores 2 per row.
- Your best score is remembered on the device you play on.
