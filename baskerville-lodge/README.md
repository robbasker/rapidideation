# Baskerville Lodge - Murder Mystery

A story game in a single web page. Open `index.html` in any browser, or on an iPad.
There is nothing to install. It is for adults: it contains murder, poisoning and
dark themes.

## The story

November 1936. A year after Sir Edmund Baskerville fell from the cliff path on
Carrach, a remote island in the Outer Hebrides, his guests are summoned back to
the lodge. You were his secretary. At dinner, the butler reads a letter Sir Edmund
left behind: he didn't fall, one of you pushed him, and the killer's name is sealed
in an envelope for the police. Then the storm closes in, the boat can't come for
three days, and the envelope disappears.

## How it plays

- **A different killer every game.** Any of the six guests can be the murderer,
  and the clues, the bodies and the slips of the tongue change to match.
- **Days.** On each day you have a few actions: search a place, search someone's
  room, or talk to someone. You can't do everything, so choose carefully.
- **Trust.** Being kind to people slowly earns their trust, and someone who trusts
  you may tell you what they saw. Pressing people hard can make the guilty slip
  up, but it costs you their goodwill. And sharing what you've found with the
  wrong person tells the killer what you know.
- **Nights.** Each night you choose whether to lock yourself in, ask someone to
  sit up with you, or creep through the house. Each choice can save a life, turn
  up a clue, or get you killed.
- **Notebook.** Every clue and every story you're told goes into your notebook,
  along with what you know about each guest.
- **The accusation.** On the last evening, or earlier if you're sure, you name
  the killer, how they killed, and why. Afterwards you see what really happened,
  and every clue you missed.

The game saves as you go, on the device you're playing on.
